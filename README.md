> Description: Vincenzo is a South Korean drama about an Italian-trained Korean lawyer who works as a consigliere for the mafia. He returns to Korea after a conflict and teams up with a sharp-witted lawyer to fight a powerful evil conglomerate using a combination of legal tactics and his own ruthless, "evil for evil" brand of justice. The show blends legal drama, action, and dark comedy as Vincenzo seeks to bring the corrupt Babel Group to justice for the people of Geumga Plaza.
